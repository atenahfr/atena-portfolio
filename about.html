About

I'm a Computer Science student at Toronto Metropolitan University, finishing my degree in 2027, and what I enjoy most is working out how a system really works underneath and then building one that holds up. That has taken me through a lot of different corners of computing: designing databases, shipping full-stack products, working in a research lab, and chasing down the problems nobody planned for.

On the engineering side, I led a team of five through two agile sprints to ship ScrumManager, a deployed project management platform, and built Log Sentinel, a log analysis dashboard that I later extended with a machine-learning model. I designed a ten-table Oracle database for a clothing retail store, from the ER diagrams down to the SQL. In the SPyCy Lab at TMU, I'm part of the team building Blueberry, a Rust file storage system that splits data across cloud providers; I designed its dashboard in Figma and am now building the frontend.

Underneath most of it is one question: what happens when real people use these systems? A thirty-minute look at network traffic once showed me a live cryptography experiment leaking its secrets in plain sight, and my thesis asks a related question about how threshold signature schemes can be designed so the secure way to use them is also the easiest. Outside of class, I work as a sales advisor at H&M, which keeps me practiced at accuracy and calm under pressure.

I'm looking for the next place to build and learn: databases, backend and systems work, or security and privacy research.
